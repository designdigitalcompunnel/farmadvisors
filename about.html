About Farm Advisors

Farmers mentoring farmers.

Farm Advisors is a mentoring program designed and led by farmers, with the goal of flattening the learning curve for regenerative agriculture practices. The program connects mentees with a growing network of farmer mentors who can provide practical advice and personalized guidance to boost farm profitability, sustainability, and resilience.

Who we are

Built around the farm, led by farmers

Our mission

Make regenerative agriculture easier to learn. Farmers who have already adopted these practices share what they know, so others can start with confidence and move faster.

Who it is for

Primary: Indiana farmers who implement conservation and regenerative agriculture practices on their farms, or who wish to.

Secondary: technical support providers such as agronomists, Extension educators, and soil conservationists.

How we are led

The Farm Advisors program manager is the main contact for mentors and mentees. Program board members and advisors give feedback as the program develops.

What we want to achieve

Two outcomes for the program

The website is built to help people take the next step: join the program, or learn about it first.

Recruit participants

Bring in farmers as mentees and experienced farmers as mentors. Clear paths, short forms, and visible next steps make it easy to say yes.

Build awareness

Help farmers, agronomists, Extension educators, and soil conservationists across Indiana know that Farm Advisors exists and what it offers.

How mentoring works

A clear path to taking part

The program manager is the main contact for mentors and mentees. These steps illustrate a simple way to understand participation.

  1. Express interest

    Tell the Farm Advisors team whether you are interested in learning from a mentor or sharing your farming experience.

  2. Connect with the program

    The program manager is the main contact and can explain the next steps for participating.

  3. Share experience

    Farmer mentors provide practical advice and personalized guidance based on their own experience.

  4. Build resilience

    The program aims to support farm profitability, sustainability, and resilience.

In partnership with

Conservation Technology Information Center (CTIC)

CTIC has a memorandum of agreement with Farm Advisors and supports the mentoring program. CTIC supports farmer-led conservation to accelerate sustainable agriculture by connecting public and private partners, informing stakeholders of national trends in soil health practices, championing practical and productive farming systems, and delivering tailored program delivery.

CTIC is committed to providing an open and accepting environment for all. CTIC prohibits any discrimination on the basis of gender, race, religion, sexual orientation, national origin, age, disability or veteran status.

Accessibility

Our commitment to everyone
